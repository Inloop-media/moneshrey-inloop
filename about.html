Why Monèshrey exists

Monèshrey started with a familiar problem: lipstick shade charts made for someone else’s skin. Nudes that turned chalky on brown skin, browns that turned orange, reds that never quite sat right.

So we started with Indian undertones and worked backwards. Every one of our eight shades is swatched on real brown lips before it makes the cut — no filters, no guesswork.

The formula had its own brief: a matte that doesn’t crack, doesn’t dry your lips out and doesn’t give up in the heat. Lightweight enough to forget you’re wearing it, and long-lasting enough to make it through chai, a commute and a wedding.

— Team Monèshrey, Ghaziabad, India
