Our story

Built around our undertones

Monèshrey started with a familiar problem: lipstick shade charts made for someone else’s skin. Nudes that turned chalky, browns that turned orange, reds that never quite sat right on brown lips.

So we started with Indian undertones and worked backwards — eight shades, each swatched on real brown lips, in a soft matte formula made for Indian weather and very long days.

— Team Monèshrey, Ghaziabad, India
